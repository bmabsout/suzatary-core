# Structural audit, 2026-09-09

The port to Rust is complete: stages 0–8 of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` are
merged, the Python is gone, and the Prodrome is its own repository. This is
the audit the owner asked for once that was done: the tree read for structure
alone, and the improvements it implies, landed as a series. Semantics are out
of scope; every change below keeps the wire, the stored format and the verb
surface byte-identical, and the tests say so.

Tree at the audit: `app/core` 18,388 lines, `app/io` 9,547, `app/cli` 5,095
plus 6,541 of integration tests; 471 public items in `core`, 130 in `io`, 69
in `cli`.

## Findings

1. **Two typst value languages.** `core/block.rs` is the hand-written parser
   and renderer ported from the Python, kept for `render_verified`;
   `core/decoration/{value,read}.rs` is the same language rendered by the
   same rules and read with `typst-syntax`. `Value`, `Arg`, `Call`, `render`
   and `render_block` exist twice. That is the "second reading of somebody
   else's language" the decoration was written to end.
   *Fix:* `render_verified` is `decoration::value::render` followed by
   `decoration::read`, compared; `block.rs` is deleted; `todos.rs` and
   `authoring.rs` read one language.
2. **`Triage` is a seam, not a capability.** It was the trait behind the
   Python launcher and still carries its shape: corpus operations (sync,
   queue, search, show, mark, noise), the document build (which `Documents`
   already is), the verification gate and a diff summary (repository
   operations), and the agent's manual and memory files. One trait, five
   concerns, and `AgentCapabilities` names it as if it were one.
   *Fix:* `Corpus` (sync, queue, mark, noise, watermark, backfill, last sync),
   `Repository` (verify, diff summary), `Manuals` (the operator's documents and
   memory); `search`/`show` move to `Mailbox`, `build_documents` to
   `Documents`. `AgentCapabilities` becomes `Chain + Corpus + Mailbox +
   Documents + Repository + Manuals + Render + Clock`, still with nothing that
   sends or resolves a peer. `io/triage.rs` becomes `io/corpus.rs`,
   `io/repository.rs` and `io/manuals.rs`.
3. **`capability.rs` is 1,100 lines.** Every trait, every newtype and their
   tests in one file.
   *Fix:* `capability/` as a directory, one file per capability, `mod.rs`
   holding the bundles and the shared newtypes (`Login`, `Secret`, `Content`).
4. **Two modules called `instance`.** `core::instance` holds the `Deployment`
   value; `cli::instance` parses `instance.toml`. Same name, different jobs.
   *Fix:* `core::deployment`.
5. **`acts.rs` is named for what it no longer is.** It was the write surface;
   since the replica it holds the add form's parsing and device registration.
   *Fix:* `core::form` for the add form, `core::devices` for registration.
6. **The binary is organised by history, not by verb.** `cli/verbs.rs` ("the
   two verbs that are not the server") holds twelve; `reading.rs`,
   `document.rs` and `mail.rs` are the stages they arrived in; `main.rs` is
   1,065 lines of clap definitions, dispatch and helpers.
   *Fix:* `cli/src/commands/<verb>.rs`, one per subcommand family (`serve`,
   `mcp`, `todo`, `todos`, `notify`, `document`, `site`, `mail`, `auth`,
   `replica`, `vault`, `verify`, `weather`, `doctor`, `fixture`), `main.rs`
   the argument tree and dispatch only.
7. **Comments narrate the port.** About forty module and item headers say
   what a thing was until stage N, or what the Python did. A reader of the
   tree now needs to know what it is.
   *Fix:* every header rewritten in the present tense; the history stays in
   git and in `CHANGELOG.md`.
8. **Hand-written wire shapes for the explanation tree.** `wire.rs` declares
   `SpecNode` and `ExplainNode` by hand because `prodrome::fpl::Explanation`
   derives neither `Serialize` nor `TS`.
   *Decision:* leave it. The explanation's JSON is a hand-built shape pinned
   by 900 vector trees; a derive would have to reproduce it byte for byte for
   no gain.
9. **Large files without an internal seam.** `core/agent.rs` (1,302 lines,
   every MCP verb), `core/site.rs` (1,202), `io/notify.rs` (951, FCM and ntfy
   in one file), `io/mailbox.rs` (1,084).
   *Fix:* `io/notify/{fcm,ntfy}.rs`; `core/agent/` split by verb family;
   `site.rs` and `mailbox.rs` left, they are one concern each.

10. **Two `raise_` functions.** `todos::raise_` and
    `decoration::block::raise_block` build the same block from a record and
    differ in three ways, one of them load-bearing: the decoration's applies
    `midnight_of` to `created` (the idempotence fix for records that carry a
    time of day) and the agent's does not. Unifying them changes rendered bytes
    for those records, so it is a decision, not a cleanup: which reading is
    right for the block a write verb hands back. Found by step 1.
    *Decision (2026-09-09):* the decoration's reading. `created` is a day by
    the record's own rule (`todo_add` writes the day of `at` since 8 September,
    and the document spells dates as days), so the block a write verb echoes
    should show the day too; a record carrying a time of day is the anomaly,
    not the block. `todos::raise_` goes and the verbs call
    `decoration::block::raise_block`. This expected one live record
    (`triageverbsstale`, completed) to render differently through the write
    verb's echo — and MEASURED, it does not: all 294 `Authored` objects render
    byte for byte identically through both paths. The block's `day` is the day
    file's `note_day`, midnight of the same `created` the renderer truncates, so
    the two move together. It would differ only if the record's raw instant were
    handed over as the file's day, which is not any file's day.

Not findings, stated so they are not re-raised: `core/site.rs`,
`core/view.rs` and `core/decoration` are three renderers of one
`prodrome::view::Entry` and belong side by side; `core/tests` is small because
the core's tests are inline; the `cli/tests` suite is the contract and its
size is right.

## Order

1 and 7 first (done 2026-09-09, step 1); then 2 and 3, with `io/triage.rs`
split as 2 says (done 2026-09-09, step 2 — with one correction: `search` and
`show` stayed with the agent's corpus rather than moving to `Mailbox`, because
they are not the reads the routes make); then 4, 5, 6, 9 (done 2026-09-09, step
3 — 6 landed as two commits, the `commands/` directory as moves and then
`main.rs` slimmed to 183 lines, with `--help` at all 36 nodes of the clap tree
diffed byte for byte across the change; `app/cli/tests/acting.rs` was renamed
`objects.rs` for the route it actually drives); then 10, with its
decision (done 2026-09-09, step 4 — `todos::raise_` deleted, `shown_of` public
as the ONE conversion, and the parity MEASURED on the live chain before the
deletion rather than predicted from the diff, which is what corrected 10's last
sentence). Each step is its own series with the gates green: `cargo fmt --check`,
clippy at `-D warnings`, `scripts/verify.sh`, the web and browser suites,
`nix build .#suzatary`, `nix flake check`.
